The Travel Mine — Phase 1 Scope (WhatsApp Lead-to-Trip Flow)

Sep 22, 2026 · @Prateek

1. Overview

Client is starting with a focused Phase 1: one complete lead-to-trip journey, proving out automated lead capture and personalized customer messaging before the rest of the requirements doc is built.

2. Phase 1 Goal

Lead comes in and is created automatically → staff work it and book manually → automated, personalized WhatsApp messages take over once a trip exists.

3. Stage 1 — Inbound WhatsApp Lead Capture

flowchart LR
    A[Customer messages\nWhatsApp] --> B{Automated menu:\nVisa / Ticketing /\nHotel / Other}
    B --> C[Customer picks\na service]
    C --> D[Form link sent\nauto-matched to service]
    D --> E[Customer fills\nform]
    E --> F[(Lead auto-created\nno staff involved)]
  • Menu options and forms are per service type, configurable
  • Every lead is timestamped and tagged with service + source

4. Stage 2 — Admin Lead Management

flowchart LR
    F[(New lead)] --> G[Staff alerted\nin-app/email/WhatsApp]
    G --> H[Staff picks up\n& contacts customer]
    H --> I{Status updated?}
    I -- No, X hrs pass --> J[Auto-reminder\nto staff]
    J --> H
    I -- Yes --> K[Status moves:\nNew -> Contacted ->\nQuoted -> Booking ->\nBooked -> Closed]
  • Reminder timing (X hrs) is configurable, not fixed
  • Leads assignable/reassignable to staff

5. Stage 3 — Manual Booking & Trip Profile Creation

Booking stays manual — software supports it, doesn't automate it.

flowchart LR
    H[Staff talks to\ncustomer] --> L[Flight suggested\n& confirmed]
    L --> M[Staff books flight\nmanually, outside system]
    M --> N[Staff enters flight\ndetails in software]
    N --> O{Hotel needed?}
    O -- Yes --> P[Staff books hotel\nmanually, enters details]
    O -- No --> Q
    P --> Q[(Trip profile\nauto-created)]
  • Trip profile forms automatically once flight/hotel details are saved — no separate step
  • Profile holds destination, arrival date/time, hotel address — enough to drive Stage 4

6. Stage 4 — Automated Customer Experience Messaging

Once a trip profile exists, messages send automatically — no staff typing.

flowchart LR
    Q[(Trip profile)] --> R[[Trigger rules\nadmin-configurable]]
    R --> S1[Pre-departure\nreminder]
    R --> S2[Arrival welcome\n+ weather + hotel distance]
    R --> S3[Any other trigger\nadmin defines]
    S1 & S2 & S3 --> T[WhatsApp message\nsent to customer]

Key requirement: triggers are configurable, not hardcoded. Admin can attach a message to any point in the trip lifecycle — any date field (departure, arrival, check-in, check-out), any status change, or a custom offset (e.g. "6 hours before departure," "1 day after return") — and edit the message template, without needing a developer to add a new trigger.

  • Arrival message needs weather + airport-to-hotel distance lookups
  • Messages personalized with customer name, flight, hotel — not generic broadcasts

7. End-to-End Flow

flowchart TD
    A[Customer messages WhatsApp] --> B[Automated menu: Visa / Ticketing / Hotel / Other]
    B --> C[Customer selects a service]
    C --> D[System sends short form link]
    D --> E[Customer fills form]
    E --> F[Lead auto-created in software]
    F --> G[Staff notified of new lead]
    G --> H[Staff follows up with customer manually]
    H --> I[System reminds staff if lead goes quiet]
    I --> J[Staff books flight/hotel manually - outside software]
    J --> K[Staff enters confirmed flight/hotel details]
    K --> L[Trip profile auto-created]
    L --> M[Pre-departure reminder sent automatically]
    L --> N[Arrival welcome message sent automatically]
    L --> O[Other trip-relevant notices sent automatically]

Stage 1 and Stage 4 are automated; Stages 2–3 are human-driven, with the system reminding and recording.

8. Out of Scope for Phase 1

The following, described in the full Software Requirements Document, are deferred to later phases:

  • Full Client Profile/CRM with complete travel history, visa history, family/group members, and communication history (Phase 1 only needs enough of a profile to support the trip and messaging)
  • Visa processing status tracking (Module 2)
  • Secure group-booking document vault (Module 4)
  • Rewards/travel points system (Module 5)
  • Bulk messaging/campaigns (Module 6)
  • Pre-travel document checklist automation (Module 8)
  • Vendor/payment follow-up tracking (Module 9)
  • Any of the Suggested Additions (client portal, payment gateway, e-signature, AI itinerary drafting, analytics dashboard, referral tracking, etc.)
  • Automated flight booking itself — booking stays a manual, human-led process in Phase 1

9. Open Questions for the Client

1. What should the WhatsApp menu options be, exactly, for Phase 1 — Visa, Ticketing, Hotel, and anything else, or just Ticketing to start?

2. What fields should the short intake form ask for per service type (e.g. for Ticketing: dates, pax, departure city, budget — anything else)?

3. How should "lead has gone quiet" be defined — after how many hours/days with no status update should staff get a reminder?

4. Who should new-lead notifications go to — a specific person, a team inbox, or whoever is assigned?

5. For the arrival weather/distance message — is a rough estimate (e.g. from the hotel address on file) acceptable, or does it need live flight-landing detection?

6. Which WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (e.g. Gupshup, Interakt, Twilio) should we use, if the client already has a preference?
